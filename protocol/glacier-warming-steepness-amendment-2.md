## Amendment 2: staged gate corrections

The v0.2 re-reviews rejected preaccess freeze but authorized continued work on
an outcome-blind frame/review-packet stage after correction. No slope,
temperature, or thickness outcome was opened.

This prospective amendment:

- separates frame/crosswalk approval from the later, separately reviewed
  outcome-analysis program;
- generates the same-GLIMS and RGI source-date review packet before review
  closure, while preaccess continues to reject every pending row;
- derives initial dependence labels from the frozen cluster table and retains
  terminal unmatched pools;
- defines exact completeness, midranks, sign-model status, finite-sample HAC
  multiplier, interval failure behavior, and an exhaustive decision truth table;
- removes unsupported IceBoost pixelwise-error propagation and stochastic
  draws, retaining deterministic morphology and `sigma_H` summaries;
- narrows the study claim to matched contrasts in each catalog attribute; and
- expands schemas for crosswalk evidence, dependence merging, missingness,
  decision statistics, and pre-/post-decode IceBoost metadata.

The outcome-blind frame and review packet may be generated after this amendment
is committed. Matching/preaccess and all outcome access remain closed until
their stated review gates are satisfied.
