## Amendment 4: participant-only primary outcome access

This amendment records the design that three independent audits approved
prospectively before systematic slope or temperature access: the exact staged
program and its typed output schema replaced the earlier full-census outcome products with the least-access design:
207 frozen participating RGI objects and only the ERA5 cell-years required by
their inherited 20-year windows. It does not change a case, control, endpoint,
contrast, dependence, completeness, or decision rule.

The access sequence is value-free request plan, label-free background features,
then label-bearing contrasts. Each transition requires an independently approved
manifest hash. The background stage computes glacier-year means and all trend
features before opening candidate, role, or component labels. Missing coordinates
or inexact calendars fail the global support/protocol gate; nonfinite participant
values propagate through the registered whole-component completeness rule.

RGI slope remains the mixed-epoch glacier-wide mean surface-slope attribute, not
pre-event source or bed slope. ERA5 remains strictly antecedent 2-m air temperature
at model orography, not glacier temperature. The result remains capped at
`DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY` and cannot establish failure probability, risk, causation, or
a warming-trigger mechanism.
